Our Story

Crafting Brilliance
Since 2018

ARK Decor was born from a passion for transforming ordinary spaces into extraordinary experiences. We believe that lighting is not merely functional — it is the soul of a room, the heartbeat of ambiance.

Our master artisans hand-select every crystal, every gilded frame, every carefully bent wire to ensure that each piece that leaves our workshop is a testament to uncompromising luxury. From intimate bedrooms to grand ballrooms, our Jhumars and lighting solutions have graced Mumbai's most prestigious homes and venues.
