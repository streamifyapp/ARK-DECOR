    ✦ OUR STORY

    Crafting Light
    Since 2012

    Born in the heart of Mumbai, ARK Decor began as a passionate pursuit of beauty in light. Over a decade later, we stand as Maharashtra's most trusted name in luxury handcrafted lighting — where every chandelier, every sconce, and every pendant tells a story of meticulous artistry.

    Our master craftsmen draw from centuries-old traditions while embracing contemporary design sensibilities. From grand hotel lobbies in South Mumbai to intimate penthouse living rooms in Bandra, ARK Decor has illuminated over two thousand distinguished spaces across India.

    Every piece is conceived with intention, crafted with precision, and delivered with care — because we believe that extraordinary light transforms not just a room, but the life lived within it.
